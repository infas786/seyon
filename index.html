About the studio

Softness, cut generously — made close to home.

SEYON started in 2016 around one cutting table and a simple idea: a housecoat should feel like the best part of your morning, not an afterthought. We still work that way — small runs, natural fibres, and a pattern block we keep refining rather than replacing every season.

Every robe and housecoat above is cut and finished by our small team, worn-tested by the women who make it, and priced for the time that actually goes into it. No filler seasons, just pieces we'd reach for ourselves, morning after morning.

— The Seyon team
